PROTOSEM — WEEK 00

The Week We Went From Strangers to a Team

5 days. A room full of unfamiliar faces. A lot of questions. And absolutely no idea how quickly those five days would turn into memories.

Every journey has a beginning.

For us, ProtoSem began with Week 0 — not with a project, a deadline, or a technical challenge, but with something much simpler:

meeting the people we were about to share this journey with.

What followed was a week of introductions, games, stories, technology, challenges, and conversations that slowly transformed a group of individuals into something that felt a little more like a team.

🌱

DAY 01

“SO… WHO ARE WE?”

The first day was about breaking the silence.

Introductions by RAM & MEERA gave everyone a first look at ProtoSem — the people, the purpose, and what the weeks ahead would look like.

But introductions can only take you so far.

Then came the Rock Paper Scissors game.

What sounds like the simplest game suddenly became an energetic elimination tournament. There was competition, laughter, unexpected alliances, and a lot of fun.

And somewhere between choosing rock, paper, or scissors, strangers started becoming teammates.

Day 1 taught us something simple: Sometimes, the fastest way to connect isn't a conversation — it's a game.

💼

DAY 02

“LET'S DISCOVER OURSELVES”

By Day 2, the room already felt a little different. We moved from simply meeting people to understanding ourselves and how we present ourselves to the world.

🔗 Building Our Professional Identity

The LinkedIn session introduced us to the importance of building a strong professional presence and preparing ourselves for professional networking.

Then came the 16Personalities test. Suddenly, it wasn't just about “What do you do?” It became: “Who are you?” The assessment helped us reflect on our working and communication styles.

📖 Our Stories, Our Voices

The short story presentations gave everyone a chance to step forward, speak, share, and express themselves. Different stories. Different personalities. Different ways of presenting.

But one common experience — standing in front of the group and being heard.

Day 2 was about discovering that before we build technology, we first need to understand the people building it — including ourselves.

⚡

DAY 03

“THINGS GOT INTERESTING”

If Day 1 helped us connect and Day 2 helped us discover, Day 3 brought everything together. Stories continued. Technology entered the conversation. And then the games began.

💻 ITEC TechTalk

An insightful ITEC TechTalk brought a technology-focused learning experience into the day. But learning didn't stop when the talk ended.

🕵️ THE IMPOSTER

Trust became the game. The Imposter team game challenged us to observe, communicate, and work together while figuring out who could be trusted.

🎯 THE CHALLENGE

Next came a fast-paced challenge where teams had to think quickly and respond on their feet.

🍡 THE MARSHMELLO GAME

And finally, the Marshmello challenge. Teams had to build while racing against the clock — turning teamwork into a hands-on challenge.

Suddenly, concepts like communication, trust, creativity, and teamwork weren't just words. We were experiencing them.

Day 3 reminded us that collaboration can be learned anywhere — even in the middle of a game.

🤖

DAY 04

“WELCOME TO THE REAL JOURNEY”

By Day 4, we were no longer simply being introduced to ProtoSem. We were beginning to understand what lay ahead.

🧠 The Art of Prompting

The Art of Prompting TechTalk introduced us to prompt engineering and how to communicate effectively with AI tools. It was a glimpse into one of the technologies and ideas that would shape our learning journey.

🎓 Orientation by Hema

Hema then took us through an orientation session covering what we needed to know as we moved forward in the programme.

🏆 THE LEADERBOARD

Then came something that instantly made things more interesting: The leaderboard. We learned how points would be tracked throughout the programme. Suddenly, the journey had a little more competition.

🚀 THE INAUGURATION

ProtoSem was officially inaugurated — marking the formal beginning of the programme.

The orientation was over. The journey had officially begun.

🌟
